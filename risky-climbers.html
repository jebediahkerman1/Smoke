Risky Climbers

Two arms. One wall. No turning back.

Height: 0 mLeft grip: readyRight grip: readyGrab higher holds to climb!

Controls: Press E to swing the currently highlighted arm around your body and grab a higher hold. Press E again to swing the other arm. Keep alternating! REGULAR gives each hand 15 seconds per grip and gold bricks break after 5 seconds. UNLIMITED is an endless mountain where you search for Sisyphus; each hand gets 20 seconds and gold bricks break after 10 seconds. SISYPHUS is also an endless mountain, but you must alternate left and right hands while dragging the boulder uphill. You cannot climb back down.
