HOW TO PLAY
• Choose an item, then click the scene to spawn it.
• Grab: drag dudes or objects around.
• Push: click to blast nearby objects away.
• Rope: click two points to connect them.
• Delete: click an object to remove it.
• Space pauses · Delete clears the selected object.
